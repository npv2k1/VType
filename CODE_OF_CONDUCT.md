# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in VType a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for
different viewpoints, and accepting responsibility. Unacceptable behavior
includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, unwanted sexual
attention, publishing private information, and conduct inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's security
reporting channel. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits,
issues, and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or
permanently ban contributors.
